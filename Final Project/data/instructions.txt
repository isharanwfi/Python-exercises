HOW TO PLAY
- Search places to find coins, clues and items. Results are random!
- Explore random places or choose where to go.
- Each place has a special action (see the menu).
- The Clinic costs 15 coins to unlock.
- A dog whistle (5 coins, sold at the Clinic) is needed to enter the Forest.
- Hint: the Park hides something under the old bench.
- Ways to earn coins: searching, recycling bottles at the Shop,
  returning lost keys, and following the map.
- Type "lopeta" in the main menu to quit.
