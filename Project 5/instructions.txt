========================================
             INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

MISSION:
Find the lost dog Duty and bring him home safely.

HOW TO PLAY:

1. Start a New Game or Continue a saved game.

2. Explore different locations:
   - Park
   - Shop
   - Neighborhood
   - Clinic
   - Forest

3. Search locations to find clues and coins.

4. Go to the Park and search the old bench.
   You may find a useful item there.

5. Collect enough coins to unlock the Clinic.

6. Visit the Clinic and buy the Dog Whistle.

7. Go to the Forest with the Dog Whistle.

8. Use the Dog Whistle to call Duty.

9. Find Duty and complete your mission!

MAIN MENU:

1. Explore
2. View Map
3. Inventory
4. View Clues
5. View Status
6. Save Game
7. Exit Game

SAVE GAME:

Choose "Save Game" to save your progress.

You can continue your saved game later
by selecting "Continue Game" when the
program starts.
