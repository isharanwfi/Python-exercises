========================================
       CALL OF DUTY - LOST DOG
========================================

Welcome to Call of Duty!

Duty, a lost dog, is somewhere in the forest.

Your mission is to find Duty and bring him home safely.

Explore different locations around the town.

Search the Park, Shop, and Neighborhood.

Collect coins and clues as you explore.

You may also find useful items that will
help you complete your mission.

Be careful! Some areas may be locked.

Find enough coins to unlock the Clinic
and get the Dog Whistle.

When you are ready, go to the Forest
and use the Dog Whistle to call Duty.

Can you find Duty?

Good luck, and have a great adventure!
